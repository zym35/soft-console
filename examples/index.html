Guidelines

Guidelines

Checkboxes and radios are small recessed wells; checked ones show an accent check or an accent ring.

Guidelines

A segmented control picks one value from two to five short options, such as a time range or a view.

A switch turns a setting on or off immediately; the knob carries a thin ring just inside its edge that turns accent when on.
